Welcome to Fantay's blog.

This is where I place my thoughts and practices.
Between code and models, between structure and whitespace, I try to find a more composed way to express technology.

I have long navigated the boundaries between engineering and intelligent systems:
From web and infrastructure, to automation and security;
From machine learning and computer vision, to AI agents and system-level collaboration.
Rather than showcasing individual technologies, I care more about how systems operate, how they constrain, and how they grow steadily in the real world.

This blog is built with Astro, supports bilingual content in Chinese and English, and pursues lightness, clarity, and long-term readability.
It is not eager to provide answers, but rather unfolds slowly like a path—documenting trials, reasoning, refinement, and ideas not yet fully formed.

I believe technology is not merely a tool for efficiency, but also an aesthetic choice.
The layers of code, the boundaries of models, the whitespace of pages—they all essentially answer the same question:
What kind of world do we wish to build.

May you find here both order and freedom.
